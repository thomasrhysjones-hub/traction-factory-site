Operator savings guarantee

Usage-priced. Volume-discounted. Capped below traditional aggregation.

Railven calculates the normal usage fee first, then applies a hard ceiling: core aggregation never exceeds 0.8% of attributable GGR/NGR for standard supported traffic profiles. Against a 1% traditional aggregation layer, that creates at least a 20% saving. High-volume operators can save more as the blended price per call falls.

The mechanism matters: Railven does not charge 0.8% revenue share. The invoice is the lower of the normal usage tariff and the 0.8% ceiling. If volume pricing produces a 0.69% equivalent, the customer pays 0.69%. The ceiling only prevents the usage bill from becoming less attractive than the core savings promise.

The model

Separate the studio, the aggregator and the infrastructure.

Game studios still earn their negotiated content share. The commercial layer Railven targets is the incremental aggregator margin, modelled here at 1%-2% of applicable GGR/NGR as a management assumption to validate. Railven prices actual usage, gives progressively cheaper calls at scale, then caps the core fee at 0.8% of attributable GGR/NGR so a standard customer still saves against a 1% aggregation layer.

Provisioning

No Railven-side integration project.

The customer may still have API or wallet work to complete on its own stack. That is different from waiting for Railven to create environments, issue credentials, configure a tenant and manually switch on providers. Standard Railven infrastructure should already know how to do that.

01 / APPLYCommercial details + payment method

Select the service, provide company/licence information and capture payment details at the point of sale.

02 / VERIFYCompliance gate

Run required corporate, licence, sanctions and market checks. Standard cases should be automated; exceptions go to human review.

03 / PROVISIONMachine-created tenant

After approval and payment authorisation, generate tenant identity, keys, endpoints, market configuration and eligible game/provider access automatically.

04 / CONNECTYour API work is yours

The customer implements Railven's published interface on its side. Railven supports the contract and platform, not a bespoke integration project.

The claim is deliberately precise: not “zero technical work anywhere”. It is zero manual Railven-side integration queue for a standard approved tenant. Legal, regulatory and security checks still happen where required.
Multi-tenancy

Shared AWS estate. Separate customer economics.

Railven is designed as pooled multi-tenant SaaS. Ten operators do not require ten isolated copies of the platform by default. The infrastructure scales as a shared service while tenant identity, access, metering and billing remain isolated.

Operator economics

Replace the aggregator margin. Not the studio.

The model separates the three parties correctly. The studio earns its negotiated content share. A traditional aggregator may add an incremental 1%-2% of GGR/NGR. Railven replaces that incremental layer with a usage-priced infrastructure bill, with a 0.8% revenue ceiling protecting the operator from losing the savings as traffic/revenue mix changes.

Studio subscriptions

A second recurring SaaS engine.

Studios keep their negotiated content economics. Railven can sell persistent access to distribution infrastructure and operating tooling on a subscription, while standard tenant provisioning remains automatic and genuine human-heavy services are priced separately.

Subscription only becomes valuable when the network is valuable.

That means early founding studios should be free or heavily discounted while operator distribution is being proven. Paid conversion is a milestone, not an assumption to force on day one.

Proof stage

The concept now needs uncomfortable evidence.

Railven is pre-product. The next stage is not catalogue scale. It is proving operator willingness to pay, technical integrity, real AWS cost per million calls, regulatory scope, enough studio supply to make the connection worth having, whether studios will convert to recurring subscriptions, and whether an approved standard customer can go from checkout to provisioned tenant without waiting on a Railven integration team.

01 / DEMANDOperator intent

Structured discovery and design-partner LOIs around transparent usage pricing.

02 / SUPPLYUseful studios

Integrate the content those operators need, then test paid subscription conversion as operator reach grows.

03 / PROVISIONINGInstant after approval

Prove automated tenant creation, credentials, eligible catalogue and billing activation with manual intervention reserved for exceptions.

04 / COGSReal unit cost

Instrument AWS cost by tenant and endpoint until the tariff is supported by production data.

What Railven is not claiming yet: a finished platform, a confirmed licence classification, a production SLA, or a validated final tariff. Those are proof-stage outputs, not website copy to invent.
