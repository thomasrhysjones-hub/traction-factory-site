Existing customers often carry more revenue than the newest sales pipeline. Yet account teams can spend more time reacting to requests than identifying where value is leaking.

We help studios, suppliers and other B2B gaming businesses make account management more commercially effective.

Four places to look first.

Good account management combines useful information, relationships and decisions. It isn't just a quarterly review deck.

01 / Portfolio

Know where revenue comes from

Segment accounts by contribution, growth potential, operational complexity and strategic value. An attractive revenue figure means less if deductions, costs or concentrated exposure are ignored.

02 / Performance

Explain changes rather than reporting them

Separate game launches, player behaviour, lobby position, promotional effects and seasonality before recommending action. Where attribution is weak, state what is unknown instead of inventing certainty.

03 / Expansion

Find credible next opportunities

Look for under-distributed products, additional brands or territories, better visibility, sensible promotions and improved commercial terms. Prioritise opportunities that an operator can justify too.

04 / Ownership

Make reviews lead to decisions

Named stakeholders, next steps, escalation paths and commercial account plans should be visible. A relationship can feel healthy while opportunities stagnate for months.

Measure the outcome.

Useful account KPIs distinguish effort from growth.

Retention and concentration

Account churn risk, revenue mix, changes in wallet share and dependency on a small number of partners.

Activation and adoption

Time to first live game or product, breadth of deployment and usage across relevant brands and markets.

Commercial expansion

Buyer-validated upsell opportunities, incremental contribution, margin and performance after promotional support ends.

Execution

Opportunity progression, completed actions, escalation time and a meaningful forecast for existing accounts.
