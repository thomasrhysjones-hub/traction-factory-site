Behind Traction Factory

Thomas Rhys Jones

Two decades leading commercial growth, partnerships and business operations across iGaming and technology.

Thomas has held CEO, director and senior commercial roles across operators, gaming suppliers, studios, affiliates and technology businesses, alongside non-executive board experience.

His work spans the commercial lifecycle: launching brands, entering new markets, building and leading teams, managing strategic partnerships and customer portfolios, and taking responsibility for revenue, profitability and delivery.

With experience across both B2B and B2C, and markets in Europe, Asia, North America, Latin America and Africa, he brings a wider perspective to complex commercial decisions. A strategy that looks good on paper still needs to work for the people, customers and partners involved.

Traction Factory applies that operating experience to businesses looking to accelerate growth, improve commercial performance or strengthen senior leadership.

What matters

Practical advice. Real ownership.

Good commercial advice should stand up to the realities of budgets, delivery teams, regulation, negotiations and demanding customers.

The best solution isn't always more sales activity or another growth initiative. Sometimes it is fixing a process, changing a proposition or deciding not to pursue a market at all.

Traction Factory exists to make those decisions clearer and help turn them into action.
