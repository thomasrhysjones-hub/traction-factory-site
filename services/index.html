What we do

Commercial support.
When it counts.

Senior commercial support for businesses facing a launch, a difficult growth problem or a leadership gap.

Sometimes that means shaping the plan. Other times it means owning the work until the job is done.

Where we step in.

Six areas. One objective: commercial progress.

01 / Strategy

Commercial strategy & go-to-market

Decide where to compete, what to sell, who to sell it to and how to get there. From market selection and pricing to launch plans and execution priorities.

Useful when: launching a product, changing direction or entering a new market.

02 / Partnerships

Strategic partnerships

Work out which partnerships can move the business forward, find the right people, shape a credible proposition and turn agreements into working relationships.

Useful when: distribution or partner access is constraining growth.

03 / Development

Business development & complex deals

Build a qualified pipeline, navigate multi-stakeholder negotiations and move opportunities from a good conversation to a signed, commercially viable agreement.

Useful when: the sales cycle is long, the stakeholders are senior or the proposition is complicated.

04 / Revenue

Revenue growth & key accounts

Understand where existing revenue is leaking or underdeveloped. Improve account planning, customer engagement, commercial terms and expansion opportunities.

Useful when: new business gets the attention but existing customers carry the revenue.

05 / Effectiveness

Commercial effectiveness

Make sales and account teams easier to run. Clear targets, useful pipeline reviews, forecasting that stands scrutiny and better ownership of the commercial process.

Useful when: activity is high but results are inconsistent.

06 / Leadership

Fractional & interim leadership

Bring experienced senior commercial ownership into the business without immediately committing to a permanent executive hire. Define the priorities, lead the team and make decisions.

Useful when: a founder needs support, an executive has left or the business is changing quickly.
